# Request for Proposal (RFP): Algorithmic Trading Analytics Platform
**Project:** MarketMatrix (Serverless Financial Data Pipeline)  
**Issuer:** Quantitative Engineering & Risk Operations — Team 2  
**Date:** May 2026

---

## 1. Introduction & Background
The **MarketMatrix** consortium is a specialized financial data and engineering team focused on quantitative trading strategies. In the rapidly evolving cryptocurrency landscape, capturing, transforming, and interpreting market volatility in real time is a critical edge. 

Currently, the team is looking to solidify and formalize its decentralized, serverless data pipeline designed to ingest raw digital asset metrics (specifically Bitcoin daily closing structures from global exchanges) and programmatically synthesize them into robust mathematical indicators. The goal is to establish a secure, enterprise-grade analytical infrastructure that seamlessly converts highly granular time-series datasets into actionable, high-conviction trading signals.

---

## 2. Objective of the RFP
The purpose of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to identify and select an advanced technical partner, cloud consultant, or specialized development team to review, optimize, deploy, and document a robust, end-to-end **Data Lakehouse** environment in the cloud. 

The selected partner will collaborate closely with our core team to ensure the underlying architecture can withstand high-frequency market updates, maintain mathematical precision across calculated indicators, and strictly protect systemic capital through automated risk guardrails.

---

## 3. Required Technical Scope
The submitted technical proposals must provide comprehensive, multi-layered strategies addressing the following foundational infrastructure pillars:

* **Data Architecture:** Refinement of a multi-tier serverless Data Lakehouse (Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers) capable of efficiently indexing, transforming, and exposing both raw unstructured transaction logs and highly structured time-series data.
* **Security & System Governance:** Rigorous implementation of identity access controls, least-privilege boundary policies, fine-grained data cataloging, and comprehensive encryption-at-rest and encryption-in-transit configurations across the cloud environment.
* **Elastic Elasticity & Scalability:** Guaranteeing an architecture that scales dynamically and vertically in real time to adapt to massive computational bursts triggered by sudden black-swan market volatility or historical asset backfilling routines without performance degradation.
* **Pipeline Automation:** Absolute adoption of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) templates (e.g., AWS CloudFormation, Terraform) to enforce predictable, version-controlled, and highly consistent deployment environments.

---

## 4. Proposal Requirements

Participating teams must structure their technical documentation responses explicitly incorporating the following four dedicated modules:

| Section | Expected Content & Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Team Profile** | Summarized descriptions of team expertise, focus areas in distributed systems or quantitative finance, and professional cloud-engineering certifications. |
| **Working Methodology** | Thorough breakdown of the execution framework (e.g., Agile/Scrum sprints) mapped to major milestones, pipeline testing routines, and technical stabilization cycles. |
| **Proposed Architecture** | In-depth technical blueprint of the suggested technology stack (justifying serverless services such as AWS Lambda, EventBridge, Glue PySpark Jobs, and Amazon Athena) coupled with cost-efficiency analysis. |
| **Budget & Timelines** | Explicit financial breakdown of cloud operational costs segmented by pipeline execution phases alongside a detailed project implementation schedule. |

---

## 5. Evaluation Process & Criteria

All submitted vendor proposals will undergo a rigorous internal vetting process evaluated against specific weighted performance metrics:
* **Technical Comprehension (40%):** Demonstrating a deep conceptual understanding of serverless data lakehouse dynamics, big data stream transformations, and complex technical indicator logic.
* **Proven Experience & Case Studies (30%):** Documented history of successfully delivering scalable cloud architectures, handling financial datasets, or stabilizing automated ETL workflows.
* **Total Implementation Cost (20%):** Evaluation of the proposed implementation budget and operational optimization strategies for data scanning.
* **Estimated Delivery Schedule (10%):** The viability, velocity, and alignment of the projected milestone schedule relative to structural academic deadlines.

No late or unformatted submissions will be integrated into the evaluation cycle.
